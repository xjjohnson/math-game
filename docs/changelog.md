# Change log

## 2026-11-07

### Community (new)
- A **Community** button on Home opens a street with a Corner shop, Bakery, Library and Park, plus School and Buddy home buttons.
- Each building has a five-question job, once a day. Shop and Bakery pay 2 coins per right answer. Library and Park give your buddy 1 heart per right answer (coins if you have no buddy). A second try the same day is practice with no pay.
- Questions get harder with grade. Kindergarten only adds within 10.
- Buildings are drawn inside too (bakery counter and oven, shop shelves and till, library bookcases, park pond and tree).

### Visit friends over Nearby (new)
- Up to 8 phones can visit the community together with the Visit button.
- Everyone in the same building shows up in the same room. Drag anyone around and every phone sees it move. Tap yourself to wave hello.
- Older app versions can still play games but cannot join a visit.

### Buy more rooms for the buddy home
- Five new rooms to buy with coins: Backyard (100), Study (120), Bedroom 2 (80), Bedroom 3 (100) and Garage (150).
- A "for sale" card shows until you buy, and you tap twice so it is never bought by accident. New rooms start empty and ready to decorate.

### New backgrounds, cards and toys
- 14 new backgrounds: Ruby Red, Cherry Red, Fire Truck, Stitch's Ohana, Bluey's Backyard, Farm, Game Day, Pirate Cove, Butterfly Garden, and holiday ones (Christmas, Valentine's, Spooky Night, Easter Spring, Fireworks).
- 16 new card styles, with little pictures in the corners (Ruby, Ocean, Outer Space, Rainbow, Stitch, Bluey, Christmas, Candy Cane, Snowflake, Halloween, Friendly Ghost, Valentine, Easter, Fireworks, Thankful, Lucky Shamrock).
- Two new shop shelves for the buddy home: Stitch (ukulele, spaceship, surfboard, hibiscus) and Bluey (Bingo plush, keepy uppy balloon, magic xylophone, Heeler sign, paw print).

### Fixes and tweaks
- Kindergarten daily challenge is now addition only. On Hard it uses two-digit addition.
- Socks and shoes on people now look right: the leg stops at the shoe and the sock is a proper sock.

### Earlier this week (Nov 1)
- Redesigned New game screen with big practice tiles and a single "More options" row.
- Clothes drawn to fit each person, take-off and hand-over by dragging.
- People hold items in their hand; more appliances and furniture in the buddy home; friendlier Stitch plus Mickey, Minnie, Olaf, Pooh, Simba and Nemo.

# Change log

## 2026-11-08 (park playground)

- **Play on the park things.** In the Park, drag yourself (or one of your buddies) onto a **swing** and you sit on it and swing. Drop yourself at the **slide** and you climb the ladder and slide down ("Wheee!"). Lift yourself into the **tree** to sit on a branch, or drop into the **pond** to swim. Drag yourself off to stop. Only one person fits on each swing.
- Works on a Nearby visit too: friends see you swinging, climbing and sliding. No new APK needed.

## 2026-11-08 (don't chase older phones)

- A host no longer gives way to a phone that hasn't updated yet. Older phones start over every 20-30 seconds and then join a host they find, so the updated phone stays the host and lets them come, instead of the two chasing each other.

## 2026-11-08 (stay the host)

- **Join or host, then stay.** Community and Several phones now work the same way: when you open them, the phone looks once for someone already hosting and joins them. If nobody is, it hosts and **stays the host**, with no more restarting every 20-30 seconds.
- **Two hosts become one.** If two phones open at the same moment and both end up hosting, they find each other and merge: the host nobody has joined yet gives way and joins the other one. A host that already has friends never gives way.
- **Several phones** no longer has Host and Join buttons: it joins a game nearby, or hosts one if there isn't one. You can still tap a game in the list while it's looking.
- Works with the current app, no new APK needed. Hosting phones put a short tag in their name so two hosts can agree which one gives way.

## 2026-11-08 (free people avatars)

- **12 free human avatars:** boys and girls, kids, teens and grown-ups, in all six skin tones and a mix of hair (Ava, Leo, Mia, Sam, Zoe, Max, Nia, Jay, Ivy, Ben, Rosa, Omar). They're drawn the same way as the people buddies, show up in the Shop's "Free avatars" row, and are also on the new-player picker. No coins needed, and they show in the Community and on visits like any other avatar. No store or protocol change.

## 2026-11-08 (later)

- **The mixer mixes.** Put the bowl on the pink stand mixer and tap the mixer (or the bowl): it whirrs, and after a moment the food turns into batter (the bowl fills with batter and says "Mixed!"). Only mixed batter bakes into a real treat; food that goes in the oven unmixed comes out as a **lumpy mess**. Adding more food after mixing means mixing again.
- **Friends' phones no longer undo your bowl.** When a phone joins a friend's visit, the things it changed in the last 10 minutes are put back the way it had them, instead of the friend's older room winning. While connected, each phone keeps up with every change, so a short drop in the connection doesn't bring back old things either.

- **Bakery bowl works the way Justin asked:** drop food on the bowl and it goes *into* the bowl (small icons on top of it) while the food itself goes straight back to its shelf to use again. The bowl keeps what's in it until it's baked or tapped to tip it out, and carrying the bowl carries it all. The big "Drop food in" and "Bake it in the oven" labels are gone; treat names are small.

- **Community things no longer reset by themselves.** A phone with no friends nearby keeps restarting its visit every 20-30 seconds to look for them, and each restart used to put everything in the buildings back on the shelves. Now the room stays as you left it, and a restarted visit starts from it. The bowl's hint is smaller ("Drop food in"), and once something is in the bowl it says "Bake it in the oven ➡️" (the oven is to the right, past the counter).

- **Bakery fixes after the first phone test:** ingredients now stay where you put them (no more springing back to the shelf); whatever sits on the bowl is in it, and carrying the bowl carries them along. They go back to their shelves only when they're used up in the oven or when you tap the bowl to tip it out. A wrong mix no longer gives a nice pudding: chocolate with the wrong things makes a **spaghetti chocolate mess** 🍝, other fruit and sweet mixes make **sticky fruit gloop**, and anything else is **weird gloop**. Salt with sweet things is always a wrong mix.

## 2026-11-08

**Bakery baking**
- In the Community **Bakery** you can now make your own treats. Drag ingredients (egg, milk, butter, strawberry, honey, salt, jam, chocolate, plus new flour, sugar, banana and apple on the wall shelf) onto the **mixing bowl** on the counter; each one springs back to its shelf and shows up above the bowl. Slide the bowl into the oven, turn the oven on, and after a few seconds out comes a treat: cakes, cookies, pies, bread, pancakes, waffles, cupcakes, croissants, pretzels, custard or fudge, depending on what you put in (flour plus butter and sugar makes cookies, add chocolate for chocolate chip). Anything else gives a yummy pudding. The treat sits on the rack with its name; tap it to eat it. Tap the bowl to tip it out and start again.
- Works on a visit: everyone sees the same bowl and treats, and anyone can add to the bowl. No protocol change (it uses the same shared pieces and switches as the oven), so phones on an older version still connect.

## 2026-11-07

**Community (the town)**
- New **Community** area: a street with a Corner shop, Bakery, Library and Park, each with a short daily job that pays coins or buddy hearts, plus a School that links to the lessons (#39).
- Community is now a **full-screen, side-view street** with doors into drawn building interiors: bakery counter and oven, shop shelves and fridge, library bookcases, park pond and tree. The street scrolls sideways like the buddy home (#44, this round).
- **Visiting friends over Nearby (up to 8 phones):** everyone in the same building stands in the same room as their character, and everyone moves their own character live (#42, #43, #44; protocol v5 and v6).
- Opening Community **connects by itself**: it joins a visit someone is already hosting, otherwise it hosts one. No Host/Join buttons.
- **Bring your buddies** (choose any of the ones you own, up to 3, from the 🐾 Bring button; nobody comes until you pick) and **wear an accessory** (hats, glasses); friends see them. You can only move your own person and buddies, never other players'.
- Money jobs are interactive: in the **Shop and Bakery** a customer pays, "Give back: ?" shows what's owed, and you tap green US dollar bills ($1, $5, $10, $20, plus $50 and $100 for older grades; no $2, no coins) onto the counter until it's the right change. Wrong answers get a gentle hint (count up for little ones, subtract for bigger kids) and a second try; kindergarten keeps the simpler add-it-up questions.
- **Bigger, hands-on buildings:** the Shop, Bakery and Library are now 3 screens long and the Park 3.4. Over 100 things can be picked up and moved, and they drop onto the shelf or counter under them. In the **Shop** you move cans, snacks, fruit and drinks between the shelves, the fridge (tap to open it), the fruit stand and the till (tap it: Ka-ching!). The **Bakery** has a mixer that whirrs when tapped, and an oven you tap on: put batter or dough inside and it bakes into a cake, cupcake or bread. In the **Library** you move books between three bookcases, there's a reading corner with a lamp, and a **computer corner** where each screen shows a quick math question (tap to see the answer, tap again for a new one). The **Park** has swings you push, a slide, a sandbox, a duck pond, a tree with apples and a squirrel, a picnic blanket, a ball, a dog, and a kite, balloon and butterfly that stay up in the sky.
- On a visit, anyone can move the things in a building and every phone sees it; the oven, mixer, swings, lamp, fridge and till are shared too.
- **Visit a friend's buddy home:** on a visit, open 👫 Friends and tap a friend's home. Their phone sends it over Nearby and you can look around every room and move things to play. Nothing you move is saved, on your phone or theirs.
- Customers pay like real people: the smallest single bill that covers the price (like $10 for $8, never $20), or the fewest bills that cover it (like $10 + $5), never exactly the price and never an extra bill they didn't need. The bubble says what it adds up to ("That's $15").
- The Community street now has the bottom tab bar (Home, Buddy, Town, Shop, Me), so you can always get back out of it.
- Jobs always pay something: the first play of the day pays full (2 coins per right answer, or hearts for your buddy at the Park) plus a bonus of 5 coins or 2 hearts; playing again the same day pays 1 coin per right answer as practice.

# Change log

## 2026-11-24 (Lolo's question streak)

- **Lolo now keeps asking until you miss.** Questions get harder as you go: two warm-ups, two just right, three challenges, then challenges from the next grade up.
- **Each right answer is worth more:** 10, 10, 15, 15, 20, 20, then 25 coins each, straight into Lolo's treasure jar. Stars at the 1st, 5th and 10th right answer. **10 in a row** ends with confetti and a 25-coin jackpot (215 coins in all).
- **A miss ends the run:** Lolo shows how the missed one works and swims off until tomorrow. Missing the very first question still pays 5 coins for finding Lolo.
- **Stop and keep my coins** after any right answer. Coins are paid as they're earned, so leaving early never loses them, and today's Lolo is used once the first question shows.
- **Best run** shows on Home under the Lolo clue and in the parent area.
- No new APK needed: tap Update now (or restart) to get it.

## 2026-11-24 (Unlimited rooms)

- **Send things to another room:** drag a toy or a buddy onto the room name at the top, then tap the room it should go to.
- The row of room buttons at the top is now one room name with arrows on each side. The Paint tab's Move buttons moved to the House map.
- Each room holds 40 things (the whole house used to hold 80), and only the room you're in and the ones next to it are drawn, so a big house stays quick.
- Rooms already bought (backyard, study, bedrooms 2 and 3, garage) are kept, and everything stays exactly where it was. Rooms still "for sale" are now built from the House map instead.
- **Visiting a big house** over Nearby: the house comes room by room, so the friend can walk in straight away; a room still on its way says "Moving in...". Nearby protocol 12: both phones need this update to visit each other's homes (a phone on this update can still visit an older phone's home).

- **Joining a friend is one tap.** Opening Community never connects or switches by itself any more. - Works with the current app (in-app update, no new APK, no protocol change).

# Change log

## 2026-11-08 (mixer buddy waits for the bowl)

- A buddy at the bakery mixer no longer grabs the bowl. It waits until you put the bowl on the mixer yourself, then mixes it a moment later.

## 2026-11-08 (real school books in the library)

- **The library's stories are now real books from Texas schools.** The 18 made-up stories on the library shelves are replaced with stories from the Texas Bluebonnet Learning readers (the state's K-5 reading program), with their real pictures: Kit, Kit's Cats, Sal's Fish Shop, Sam and the Duck and Max in the Mud (kindergarten); Where Is King?, Splash Dogs, The Fox and the Grapes, The Fox and the Crane, Big Cats, The Frog Jumping Contest and A Rainforest Ride (1st grade); The Hare and the Hedgehog, The Pancake and The Math Contest (2nd grade); and three chapter books: Alice's Adventures in Wonderland (3rd), Treasure Island (4th) and The Badlands Sleuth (5th).
- Picture books show the printed pages as an open book. Chapter books show the words as big text with the book's pictures. Read to me reads every page. A long book opens where you left off.
- The book shows its grade next to its title, and the last page credits the Texas Education Agency (the readers are free to share under Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0).
- The secret bakery recipe books are still on the shelves, in the same places.
- No new APK needed; works with the in-app Update now (a bigger download than usual, about 17 MB of pictures).

## 2026-11-08 (buddy hangs off the mixer)

- **Put a buddy on the bakery mixer.** Drag one of your buddies up onto the stand mixer and it grabs the end of the mixer's arm and hangs there, swaying, with "I'll mix!". Put food in the bowl and it pulls the bowl under itself and runs the mixer, swinging back and forth while it whirrs ("Mixing! 🥄", then "All mixed! 👍"). Standing a buddy by the mixer still works too.
- No new APK needed; works with the in-app Update now.

## 2026-11-08 (people and buddies come alive in Community)

- **Same bodies as at home.** In Community your buddies look just like they do in the buddy home: Bluey is blue, the pup wears its crown, and people buddies (Mom, Dad, sisters...) and people avatars are whole drawn people instead of a face on a cartoon body. A friend's buddies come in their own colors and starting clothes.
- **Everyone moves like at home.** People and buddies breathe, people blink, they walk (with a little waddle) to wherever they're put instead of jumping, friends' moves glide too, waving makes them hop and wave an arm, and putting someone down makes them land with a squash. Things you move slide into place.
- **A buddy runs the bakery mixer.** Stand one of your buddies by the mixer and it says "I'll mix!". Put food in the bowl and, a moment after the last thing goes in, it pulls the bowl onto the mixer and mixes it, rocking along ("Mixing! 🥄", then "All mixed! 👍"). Tapping the mixer yourself still works.
- **Home:** your character and buddy breathe on the Home street; tap your character to make it hop.
- **One plan for animation:** docs/animation.md lists the moves every screen uses and what's next.
- No new APK needed; works with the in-app Update now. Animations stop when Animations is off in Settings.

## 2026-11-08 (buddies come along on visits)

- **You and your buddies come along when you visit a friend's home.** Walk into a friend's buddy home on a Community visit and you're standing just inside their front door, with the buddies you picked in **🐾 Bring** next to you. Never picked any? Your buddy comes along anyway.
- Your buddies look just like they do at home: same body colors, same clothes, people buddies with their hair and outfits.
- Drag yourself and your buddies anywhere in their home, even onto seats. Tap yourself and it says hi. Like everything else on a visit, it all goes back when you leave.
- Only on your phone for now (your friend's phone doesn't show you in their home). No new APK needed and no protocol change.

## 2026-11-08 (closes after 30 minutes in the background)

- **The app closes itself after 30 minutes in the background** on Android. If you switch away (or the screen turns off) and don't come back within 30 minutes, it hangs up any Nearby connection, leaves the Recents list and stops, so it can't keep the radio, sounds or timers going and drain the battery. Coming back within 30 minutes carries on where you left off.
- Players, coins and homes are saved as they change, so nothing is lost; only a round that was left half-way through starts over.
- If the phone is fully asleep it closes within a minute or so of waking up.
- PC/web is unchanged.
- **Needs the new APK** (small native change). No protocol or store version change; older phones still connect.

## 2026-11-08 (paint easel)

- **The easel in the School's Color corner works.** Tap it and it opens full screen. Paint with your finger on blank paper: 13 paint colors, thin, medium and thick brushes, **Rub out**, a **paint bucket** (Fill), **Undo** (takes back your own last line or fill) and **New** paper.
- **8 coloring pages:** house, flower, rocket, fish, cat, turkey, pumpkin and butterfly. Tap an area with the paint bucket to fill it, or paint over it; the black outlines always stay on top.
- **Color by number:** pick any page with Easy (3 + 4), Medium (12 − 5) or Hard (21 ÷ 3) sums (it starts at the level for the player's grade). Each area shows a sum; the answer is the number on the paint to use. **Check** says how many are left and makes the wrong ones flash red; get them all and "You did it!".
- **My pictures:** **Save** keeps a picture (up to 12 each). Open one again to keep painting, throw it away, or **Hang** it: it goes up as a framed picture on the wall of your buddy's home (drag it where you like; tap it and your buddy loves it).
- **Paint together:** on a Community visit everyone paints on the same paper and sees it live, and a friend who joins later sees the picture so far. The easel in the room shows what is on it.
- **Smoother, faster chalkboard:** each chalk line is now drawn as one smooth line instead of lots of little pieces, so a full board doesn't slow down.
- **Needs the new APK** (it adds a drawing library). Protocol v10, but older phones can still visit; they just paint on their own.

## 2026-11-08 (safer saving, steadier Nearby)

- **Saved players can't be wiped by an update.** Before an update changes the saved players, a copy is kept (the last 3). If the saved players ever fail to open, the app stops saving instead of saving an empty list over them, Home says to ask a grown-up, and the Parent area has a **Saved players** card with **Restore copy from (date)** (tap twice). The card only shows when there is a copy or a problem.
- **Steadier Nearby connections.** Once a phone has joined a host, it stops searching for other phones, and a host stops searching once a friend has joined (it looks again if everyone leaves). Searching uses the radio heavily and made connections slow and drop out. Applies to Community visits and games on several phones.
- New tests: upgrading saved players from every old version (1 to 13), and the backup and restore.
- Needs the new APK (small native change). No protocol or store version change; older phones still connect.

## 2026-11-08 (farm)

- **New Farm building**, the sixth on the street (after the park; the street scrolls a little further). Inside: a red barn with a hay loft and open doors, a silo, a hen house with nest boxes, an animal pen with a mud puddle, a duck pond, a vegetable patch, a windmill (tap to spin) and a tractor.
- **Farm animals:** horse, cow, cat, rooster, three hens, two chicks, pig, sheep, goat, turkey, two ducks, dog and a bunny. Drag them anywhere (several fingers at once, like the other buildings). **Tap an animal** and it hops and says "Moo!", "Oink oink!", "Cock-a-doodle-doo!" and so on in a bubble, out loud too when Sounds are on. Put the pig (or anyone) in the **mud** and it rolls about; put an animal in the **pond** and it bobs. The animals breathe gently.

Tap the hen house to put eggs in your basket: single eggs, boxes of 10 (1st grade up), crates of 100 (3rd grade up), trucks of 1000 (5th grade up). Single eggs line up in fives so they are easy to count. Tap one in the basket to put it back. A hint and a second try before the answer is shown. Kindergarten counts and adds only. Pays coins.
- **Needs the new APK on every phone** (protocol v9). A phone that hasn't updated is asked to update before it can visit the Community.

## 2026-11-08 (don't chase older phones)

## 2026-11-08 (stay the host)

- **Join or host, then stay.** Community and Several phones now work the same way: when you open them, the phone looks once for someone already hosting and joins them. If nobody is, it hosts and **stays the host**, with no more restarting every 20-30 seconds.
- **Two hosts become one.** If two phones open at the same moment and both end up hosting, they find each other and merge: the host nobody has joined yet gives way and joins the other one. A host that already has friends never gives way.
- **Several phones** no longer has Host and Join buttons: it joins a game nearby, or hosts one if there isn't one. You can still tap a game in the list while it's looking.
- Works with the current app, no new APK needed. Hosting phones put a short tag in their name so two hosts can agree which one gives way.

## 2026-11-08 (later)

- **Several fingers at once in the Community buildings and street**, like the buddy home: hold something with one finger and slide the room along with another, carry two things at once, or hold a thing at the side of the screen to scroll that way. Works for your person, your buddies and every movable thing.

- **The mixer mixes.** Put the bowl on the pink stand mixer and tap the mixer (or the bowl): it whirrs, and after a moment the food turns into batter (the bowl fills with batter and says "Mixed!"). Only mixed batter bakes into a real treat; food that goes in the oven unmixed comes out as a **lumpy mess**. Adding more food after mixing means mixing again.
- **Friends' phones no longer undo your bowl.** When a phone joins a friend's visit, the things it changed in the last 10 minutes are put back the way it had them, instead of the friend's older room winning. While connected, each phone keeps up with every change, so a short drop in the connection doesn't bring back old things either.

- **Bakery bowl works the way Justin asked:** drop food on the bowl and it goes *into* the bowl (small icons on top of it) while the food itself goes straight back to its shelf to use again. The bowl keeps what's in it until it's baked or tapped to tip it out, and carrying the bowl carries it all. The big "Drop food in" and "Bake it in the oven" labels are gone; treat names are small.

- **Community things no longer reset by themselves.** A phone with no friends nearby keeps restarting its visit every 20-30 seconds to look for them, and each restart used to put everything in the buildings back on the shelves. Now the room stays as you left it, and a restarted visit starts from it. The bowl's hint is smaller ("Drop food in"), and once something is in the bowl it says "Bake it in the oven ➡️" (the oven is to the right, past the counter).

- **Bakery fixes after the first phone test:** ingredients now stay where you put them (no more springing back to the shelf); whatever sits on the bowl is in it, and carrying the bowl carries them along. They go back to their shelves only when they're used up in the oven or when you tap the bowl to tip it out. A wrong mix no longer gives a nice pudding: chocolate with the wrong things makes a **spaghetti chocolate mess** 🍝, other fruit and sweet mixes make **sticky fruit gloop**, and anything else is **weird gloop**. Salt with sweet things is always a wrong mix.

## 2026-11-08

**Bakery baking**
- In the Community **Bakery** you can now make your own treats. Drag ingredients (egg, milk, butter, strawberry, honey, salt, jam, chocolate, plus new flour, sugar, banana and apple on the wall shelf) onto the **mixing bowl** on the counter; each one springs back to its shelf and shows up above the bowl. Slide the bowl into the oven, turn the oven on, and after a few seconds out comes a treat: cakes, cookies, pies, bread, pancakes, waffles, cupcakes, croissants, pretzels, custard or fudge, depending on what you put in (flour plus butter and sugar makes cookies, add chocolate for chocolate chip). Anything else gives a yummy pudding. The treat sits on the rack with its name; tap it to eat it. Tap the bowl to tip it out and start again.
- Works on a visit: everyone sees the same bowl and treats, and anyone can add to the bowl. No protocol change (it uses the same shared pieces and switches as the oven), so phones on an older version still connect.

- Community is now a **full-screen, side-view street** with doors into drawn building interiors: bakery counter and oven, shop shelves and fridge, library bookcases, park pond and tree. The street scrolls sideways like the buddy home (#44, this round).
- **Visiting friends over Nearby (up to 8 phones):** everyone in the same building stands in the same room as their character, and everyone moves their own character live (#42, #43, #44; protocol v5 and v6).

Wrong answers get a gentle hint (count up for little ones, subtract for bigger kids) and a second try; kindergarten keeps the simpler add-it-up questions.
- **Bigger, hands-on buildings:** the Shop, Bakery and Library are now 3 screens long and the Park 3.4. Over 100 things can be picked up and moved, and they drop onto the shelf or counter under them. In the **Shop** you move cans, snacks, fruit and drinks between the shelves, the fridge (tap to open it), the fruit stand and the till (tap it: Ka-ching!). The **Bakery** has a mixer that whirrs when tapped, and an oven you tap on: put batter or dough inside and it bakes into a cake, cupcake or bread. In the **Library** you move books between three bookcases, there's a reading corner with a lamp, and a **computer corner** where each screen shows a quick math question (tap to see the answer, tap again for a new one). The **Park** has swings you push, a slide, a sandbox, a duck pond, a tree with apples and a squirrel, a picnic blanket, a ball, a dog, and a kite, balloon and butterfly that stay up in the sky.

- **Visit a friend's buddy home:** on a visit, open 👫 Friends and tap a friend's home. Their phone sends it over Nearby and you can look around every room and move things to play. Nothing you move is saved, on your phone or theirs.
- Customers pay like real people: the smallest single bill that covers the price (like $10 for $8, never $20), or the fewest bills that cover it (like $10 + $5), never exactly the price and never an extra bill they didn't need. The bubble says what it adds up to ("That's $15").
- The Community street now has the bottom tab bar (Home, Buddy, Town, Shop, Me), so you can always get back out of it.
- Jobs always pay something: the first play of the day pays full (2 coins per right answer, or hearts for your buddy at the Park) plus a bonus of 5 coins or 2 hearts; playing again the same day pays 1 coin per right answer as practice.

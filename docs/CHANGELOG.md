# Change log

## 2026-11-07

**Community (the town)**
- New **Community** area: a street with a Corner shop, Bakery, Library and Park, each with a short daily job that pays coins or buddy hearts, plus a School that links to the lessons (#39).
- Community is now a **full-screen, side-view street** with doors into drawn building interiors: bakery counter and oven, shop shelves and fridge, library bookcases, park pond and tree. The street scrolls sideways like the buddy home (#44, this round).
- **Visiting friends over Nearby (up to 8 phones):** everyone in the same building stands in the same room as their character, and everyone moves their own character live (#42, #43, #44; protocol v5 and v6).
- Opening Community **connects by itself**: it joins a visit someone is already hosting, otherwise it hosts one. No Host/Join buttons.
- **Bring your buddies** (choose any of the ones you own, up to 3, from the 🐾 Bring button; nobody comes until you pick) and **wear an accessory** (hats, glasses); friends see them. You can only move your own person and buddies, never other players'.
- Money jobs are interactive: in the **Shop and Bakery** a customer pays, "Give back: ?" shows what's owed, and you tap green US dollar bills ($1, $5, $10, $20, plus $50 and $100 for older grades; no $2, no coins) onto the counter until it's the right change. Wrong answers get a gentle hint (count up for little ones, subtract for bigger kids) and a second try; kindergarten keeps the simpler add-it-up questions.
- **Bigger, hands-on buildings:** the Shop, Bakery and Library are now 3 screens long and the Park 3.4. Over 100 things can be picked up and moved, and they drop onto the shelf or counter under them. In the **Shop** you move cans, snacks, fruit and drinks between the shelves, the fridge (tap to open it), the fruit stand and the till (tap it: Ka-ching!). The **Bakery** has a mixer that whirrs when tapped, and an oven you tap on: put batter or dough inside and it bakes into a cake, cupcake or bread. In the **Library** you move books between three bookcases, there's a reading corner with a lamp, and a **computer corner** where each screen shows a quick math question (tap to see the answer, tap again for a new one). The **Park** has swings you push, a slide, a sandbox, a duck pond, a tree with apples and a squirrel, a picnic blanket, a ball, a dog, and a kite, balloon and butterfly that stay up in the sky.
- On a visit, anyone can move the things in a building and every phone sees it; the oven, mixer, swings, lamp, fridge and till are shared too.
- **Visit a friend's buddy home:** on a visit, open 👫 Friends and tap a friend's home. Their phone sends it over Nearby and you can look around every room and move things to play. Nothing you move is saved, on your phone or theirs.
- Customers pay like real people: the smallest single bill that covers the price (like $10 for $8, never $20), or the fewest bills that cover it (like $10 + $5), never exactly the price and never an extra bill they didn't need. The bubble says what it adds up to ("That's $15").
- The Community street now has the bottom tab bar (Home, Buddy, Town, Shop, Me), so you can always get back out of it.
- Jobs always pay something: the first play of the day pays full (2 coins per right answer, or hearts for your buddy at the Park) plus a bonus of 5 coins or 2 hearts; playing again the same day pays 1 coin per right answer as practice.
